## Not provided

These are not planned here; [Substrate](https://beyond10x.github.io/substrate/)
([GitHub](https://github.com/beyond10x/substrate)) provides them.

- cgroup bounds and quotas for CPU, memory, processes and storage;
- a seccomp filter;
- an API, a daemon, or a record of what ran.

## Known limitations

- **Linux only.** The tests run on Linux; nothing is known about other systems.
- **No option to pass environment variables.** Use `env NAME=value` in front of the command
  ([Run a project's tests in the sandbox](/docs/guides/run-tests-in-the-sandbox)), or
  `Options::env` from the library.
- **No `/etc`.** Programs that need DNS resolution, TLS certificates or user names do not find
  their files under bubblewrap.
